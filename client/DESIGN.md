# SiteCreator — Design Specification

## Incumbent Visual Identity
- **Font**: `Suisse Intl`, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, sans-serif
- **Palette**: `#171719` (ink text), `#595a60` (muted), `#ffffff` (background), `#f0f3ff` (lilac surface), `#edf8fa` (blue preview surface), `#dedfe5` (lines & borders), `#d94f00` (orange primary accent).
- **Header**: Persistent left sidebar header (`236px` on desktop) with `<span>sc</span>SiteCreator` logo mark and clean navigation links.
- **Components**: `.welcome` banner, `.search-area` form, `.results` lead listing, `.lead` items, `.pill` status badges, `footer`.
